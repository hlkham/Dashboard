Pivot bars (daily)
Bars needed on each side of a pivot

Healthy: price above the 50 and 200 day and week averages, day 50 above 200, and no lower-high / lower-low structure. Unhealthy: price below the 200 day, or below the 50 day while the structure is a downtrend. Caution: everything in between. Structure uses 20-bar and support / resistance 5-bar daily pivots (set above). BMSB is the weekly 20-period SMA / 21-period EMA band (Bull Market Support Band). Not financial advice.
