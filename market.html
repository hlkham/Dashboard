Connection settings (set up your own free proxy for reliable prices)

Yahoo Finance blocks most public relays, so prices can fail (HTTP 401/403). A free Cloudflare Worker of your own fixes this permanently and takes about 3 minutes:

  1. Sign in at dash.cloudflare.com, open Workers & Pages, click Create, then Hello World, then Deploy.
  2. Click Edit code, replace everything with the contents of price-proxy-worker.js, then click Deploy.
  3. Copy the Worker address (it ends in .workers.dev) and paste it below.
